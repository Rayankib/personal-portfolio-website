Frontend Development / Multiplayer Game / Team Project

Skraw.io

A browser-based drawing-and-guessing game developed as a team project. I focused on early frontend structure, canvas interaction and UX/UI exploration.

This is a team project, not a solo-built game. Multiplayer infrastructure and game functionality were developed collaboratively.

Runtime validation

Restoring and testing the original build

I restored the historical project locally and tested it using two browser sessions. The gameplay screenshot records the real application during that multiplayer test.

  • Multiple clients joined the same lobby, with player and ready-state updates synchronized.
  • Freehand drawing and normal chat messages synchronized between clients.
  • Correct guesses were recognized; the drawer saw the word while guessers saw placeholders.

Transparency note: In the restored historical build, the timer reached zero without advancing the round, language selection was not applied to gameplay, the AI-helper service was unavailable, and some advanced drawing-tool selections produced runtime errors.

My role

My frontend contribution

I focused on frontend development and UX/UI within the wider team project. My contribution is distinct from the later shared game and backend implementation.

  • I created the initial component-based game interface and early App.vue layout.
  • I established an early CanvasBoard.vue drawing baseline and drawing controls.
  • I worked on the initial GameHeader.vue, PlayerList.vue, and general frontend styling.
  • I researched and designed the timer experience, and contributed to an early timer implementation/prototype.
  • I used user feedback and questionnaire work to validate interface and timer concepts.

The countdown shown in the gameplay screenshot belongs to the later team build. I do not claim authorship of its current implementation.
